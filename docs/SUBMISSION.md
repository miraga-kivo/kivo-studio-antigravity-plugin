## Three proposed user journeys

These are demonstration plans, not claims of completed tests.

### 1. Original city launch trailer

A creator supplies an original city, districts, character, ratio and budget. City Cinematics makes a fictional concept map and a shot plan locally. The creator approves current Kivo quotes, compares video Drafts and selects a native final; editable graphics and a final cut are returned. Kivo keeps the prompt/assets/jobs visible in the same project.

### 2. Camera/blocking review before model spend

A director describes an alley orbit or meeting/hug/crane shot. White-model Video creates seekable Three.js previs, opens a local HyperFrames preview and renders an inspected MP4. After revision approval and upload authorization, the same MP4 becomes a Kivo reference. Appearance references remain separate from camera/timing guidance; paid generation needs its own approved quote.

### 3. Antigravity Agent Manager film coordination

When parallel agents are requested and supported, one coordinator assigns separate shots and documents through Antigravity Agent Manager. A writer owns dialogue, a scene artist owns references, and an editor owns EDL/QC; a single submission coordinator serializes quotes, budgets and mutable project changes. Agents hand off versioned artifacts and selected Draft IDs, then the coordinator prepares approved native finals and an inspected edit. In a single-agent environment, the same workflow uses roles as responsibilities without requiring subagents.
